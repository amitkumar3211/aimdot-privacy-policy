Privacy Policy

Custom Crosshair: AimDot (Android) · Last updated: 18 November 2026

Custom Crosshair: AimDot ("AimDot", "the app", "we") is a visual customisation tool that draws a user-chosen crosshair on top of other apps. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: AimDot works offline, has no accounts, and does not upload your screen, your games, or your personal data anywhere.

Summary
  • No account or sign-in. No personal information is requested.
  • Your crosshair designs, game presets and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Dynamic mode analyses your screen on the device, in memory only, and only while you have explicitly turned it on. Frames are never saved or transmitted.
  • AimDot does not read game memory, inject code into games, or automate gameplay.

3. Dynamic mode and screen capture

Dynamic mode is an optional, experimental feature. When you turn it on, Android asks you to allow screen sharing with AimDot. The app then:

  • receives a reduced-resolution copy of the screen while the feature is running;
  • analyses each frame on your device, in memory, to find the game's visible aiming reticle and move the AimDot indicator to it;
  • discards every frame immediately after analysis.

Screen frames are never stored on disk, never sent to us or to any third party, and never used for analytics. The only thing kept is the small calibration pattern described in section 1. Capture stops when you tap Stop, when the notification is dismissed, when you revoke sharing in Android, or when the app is closed.

Because the screen is shared, anything visible on it during that time is processed by the app in the same way. We recommend turning Dynamic mode off before opening apps that show private information. Dynamic mode only mirrors the reticle that the game already draws; it does not detect other players, read game data, or interact with the game.

4. Information we do not collect

AimDot does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers for tracking, or gameplay screenshots. The app has no server of its own.

5. Third-party services

The app may include the following services from Google. Each has its own privacy policy, and some may collect device information such as an advertising ID, app version, or crash details.

  • Google Play Billing – to process optional in-app purchases (AimDot Pro). We do not see or store your payment details. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob – to show ads in the free version on browsing screens only, never over gameplay or the overlay. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. AdMob privacy
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics – anonymous usage events (for example "overlay started") and crash reports to improve stability. No screen content is ever included. Firebase privacy

If a version of the app does not include one of these services, that service does not apply to it.

6. Children

AimDot is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

All app data stays in the app's private, sandboxed storage on your device, which other apps cannot read without root access.

8. Your choices

  • Revoke "Display over other apps" or stop screen sharing at any time in Android settings.
  • Delete all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling.
  • Opt out of personalised ads through your device's Google settings.
