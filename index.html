Privacy Policy

Custom Crosshair: AimDot (Android) · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Custom Crosshair: AimDot ("AimDot", "the app", "we") is a visual customisation tool that draws a user-chosen crosshair on top of other apps. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: AimDot works offline, has no accounts, and does not upload your screen, your games, or your personal data anywhere.

Summary
  • No account or sign-in. No personal information is requested.
  • Your crosshair designs, game presets and settings are stored only on your device.
  • AimDot never captures your screen, reads game memory, injects code into games, or automates gameplay.

1. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following data locally in its private storage. It never leaves the device unless you back it up yourself (for example via Android's app backup).

DataPurpose
Crosshair designs you create or favouriteSo you can reuse and edit them
Game presets (name, chosen crosshair, on-screen position)Different settings per game
App settings (mode, overlay behaviour, premium status)Remembering your preferences

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from Android Settings → Apps → AimDot → Storage → Clear data.

2. Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)To draw the crosshair on top of your game. Requested only when you start the overlay; you can revoke it in Android settings at any time.
Foreground service and notificationsTo keep the overlay running while a game is in front and to show a persistent notification with Pause and Stop controls.

3. Information we do not collect

AimDot does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers for tracking, or any screenshots or screen content. The app has no server of its own.

4. Third-party services

The app may include the following services from Google. Each has its own privacy policy, and some may collect device information such as an advertising ID, app version, or crash details.

  • Google Play Billing – to process optional in-app purchases (AimDot Pro). We do not see or store your payment details. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob – to show ads in the free version on browsing screens only, never over gameplay or the overlay. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. AdMob privacy
  • RevenueCat – manages AimDot Pro subscriptions and restores purchases. It receives an anonymous app user id and purchase receipts from Google Play, never your name or payment details. RevenueCat privacy
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics – anonymous usage events (for example "overlay started") and crash reports to improve stability. Firebase privacy

If a version of the app does not include one of these services, that service does not apply to it.

5. Children

AimDot is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

All app data stays in the app's private, sandboxed storage on your device, which other apps cannot read without root access.

7. Your choices

  • Revoke "Display over other apps" at any time in Android settings.
  • Delete all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling.
  • Opt out of personalised ads through your device's Google settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
